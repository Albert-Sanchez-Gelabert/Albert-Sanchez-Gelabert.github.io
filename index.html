Albert Sánchez-Gelabert

Sociologist · Lecturer · Researcher

Institut de Ciències de l’Educació (ICE) · Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya · BarcelonaTech (UPC)

I am a sociologist whose research focuses on social inequalities in education, particularly in higher education. I study how social background, educational pathways and institutional contexts shape access to university, degree choice, persistence, dropout and students’ academic trajectories.

Much of my work uses quantitative and longitudinal approaches to examine how inequalities develop over time and across educational transitions. More recently, I have broadened my research to life-course inequalities and cultural consumption, including digital participation, musical tastes and processes of social differentiation.
