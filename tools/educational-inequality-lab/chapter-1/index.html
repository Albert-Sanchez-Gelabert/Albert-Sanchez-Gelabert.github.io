05 / Your sociological insight

Educational opportunities are shaped before university.

The discovery
A smaller gap between groups does not necessarily mean higher opportunities for either group.

The study shows how predicted transitions vary by migrant background, gender and school composition. Understanding inequality requires examining both the distance between groups and the levels of opportunity available to them.

You have now seen why a smaller group difference does not, by itself, indicate better opportunities.
