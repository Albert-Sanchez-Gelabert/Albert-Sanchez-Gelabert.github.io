Online study can ease travel and fixed attendance requirements. It cannot eliminate competing responsibilities or differences in available resources. Those are plausible sociological mechanisms, not causes directly identified by this study.

03 / Your sociological insight

More ways to study do not guarantee equal chances to finish.

The discovery

Flexibility can help students remain in higher education. It does not remove all the inequalities that shape their outcomes.

Online university can make continued enrolment possible for students whose circumstances do not fit conventional attendance. Yet many are still studying after years of enrolment, and the likelihood of leaving without a degree remains associated with family background.

Educational opportunities are not simply about having an alternative. They also depend on the conditions under which students can make use of it.

Across Beyond merit, the story is about how educational opportunities are shaped, reconsidered and experienced across successive decisions — rather than by merit or effort alone.
