## Editorial notes

This is an **editorial draft**, not a finished scientific review. It is built from the accepted manuscript of Sánchez-Gelabert & Simarro Rodríguez (2026):
https://albert-sanchez-gelabert.github.io/files/Gender_STEM_AAM.pdf
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/02635143.2026.2727576

All numeric values presented in the study come from the article: 57,159 applicants, 36,075 first-time entrants, 23 AQU Catalunya subfields, the STEM/non-STEM 11,463/24,612 split, the MCA factor contributions and the 8-cluster shares. The small methodological arrows and chips are **schematic**, not synthetic MCA observations. No gender-by-subfield proportions are invented.

### What each section opens up

00 Overview — article and editorial premise.
01 The question — from the gender participation gap to entry configurations; three research questions.
02 Previous work — interests, disciplinary cultures and educational stratification; remaining gap.
03 The concepts — beyond gender through intersecting social/academic trajectories (with a non-causality caveat).
04 The data — applicant file, cohort restrictions, scope and missing subjective dimensions.
05 The measure — AQU 23-subfield classification and STEM boundary (with real counts).
06 The methodological strategy — MCA active/illustrative distinction, factors, contributions, Ward clusters, alternative regression question.
07 The conclusions — academic over-selection, field heterogeneity, limitations and further research.
